# Customer Service Representative - Career Opportunity

> Join Oversea Staffing Solutions as a Customer Service Representative in our Caribbean talent hub.

## Position Overview
As a Customer Service Representative, you will handle inbound calls, emails, and live chat inquiries from international clients, providing friendly, efficient, and professional support.

## Key Responsibilities
- Assist customers with account management, order status, and general product inquiries.
- Document customer interactions accurately in CRM software.
- Maintain high customer satisfaction (CSAT) scores and adhere to client SLA guidelines.
- Escalate complex technical or billing issues to Tier 2 support when necessary.

## Qualifications & Skills
- Excellent verbal and written fluency in English (French or Spanish is a major plus).
- Strong interpersonal skills and empathetic customer orientation.
- Computer literacy and ability to learn CRM and ticketing tools quickly.
- High school diploma or university background.
