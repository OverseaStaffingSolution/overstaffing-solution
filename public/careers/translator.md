# Bilingual Translator & Interpreter - Career Opportunity

> Join our team of professional language specialists providing translation and localization services for global clients.

## Position Overview
Provide accurate written translation and real-time interpretation across English, French, Spanish, and Haitian Creole for business communications, technical documents, and customer service channels.

## Key Responsibilities
- Translate documents, emails, chat logs, and marketing materials between target languages.
- Maintain consistency in industry terminology and cultural nuances.
- Assist support teams with live multilingual calls when required.

## Qualifications & Skills
- Native or near-native fluency in English and French or Spanish.
- Formal translation experience or relevant language certifications preferred.
- High attention to detail and speed in written translation.
