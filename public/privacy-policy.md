# Privacy Policy

> Oversea Staffing Solutions Privacy Policy & Candidate Data Protection.

## Overview
Oversea Staffing Solutions ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, submit contact forms, or apply for job openings.

## Data Collection
- **Contact Forms**: Name, email address, phone number, and message content submitted voluntarily.
- **Job Applications**: Candidate name, email, phone number, resume data, and cover letter files.
- **Automated Data**: Standard web analytics, IP address, and browser cookies used strictly for security and performance optimization.

## Data Protection & Use
- We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties.
- All candidate data and contact requests are encrypted and stored in secure, access-controlled databases.
- You may request data deletion or modification at any time by contacting contact@overseastaffingsolutions.com.
