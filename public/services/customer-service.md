# Customer Support & Service

> Dedicated inbound and outbound customer support teams delivering empathetic, fast, and multilingual assistance.

## Key Features
- **24/7/365 Coverage**: Round-the-clock availability for global customers.
- **Omnichannel Support**: Seamless integration across Phone, Email, SMS, Social Media, and Ticketing systems (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Salesforce).
- **Multilingual Agents**: Native-level English, French, and Spanish customer representatives.
- **Quality Assurance**: Continuous performance monitoring, SLA adherence, and CSAT optimization.

## Ideal Use Cases
- E-commerce customer care and order management.
- Subscription billing and account inquiries.
- Service desk desk operations and escalation management.
