# Live Chat & Messaging Support

> Instant, real-time web and app chat support to boost website conversions and resolve issues immediately.

## Key Features
- **Rapid Response Times**: Under 30-second average response rate to visitor inquiries.
- **Sales & Support Dual Capability**: Guiding visitors through purchase decisions and answering product questions.
- **Co-Browsing & Ticket Escalation**: Helping users navigate web platforms directly.
- **Integration with AI Chatbots**: Human-in-the-loop takeover when AI bots require escalation.

## Ideal Use Cases
- High-traffic e-commerce storefronts.
- SaaS onboarding and free trial conversion.
- Healthcare and financial customer portals.
