# Market Research & Data Collection

> Actionable market intelligence, customer feedback surveys, and competitor analysis conducted by trained researchers.

## Key Features
- **Phone & Web Surveys**: CATI (Computer-Assisted Telephone Interviewing) surveys and web-based feedback.
- **Competitor Benchmarking**: Gathering structured data on pricing, service features, and market positioning.
- **Customer Satisfaction (NPS/CSAT)**: Systematically evaluating customer loyalty and product feedback.
- **Structured Reporting**: Delivering clear visual summaries, raw datasets, and executive insights.

## Ideal Use Cases
- Pre-launch product validation.
- Customer sentiment and churn analysis.
- Industry trends and pricing research.
