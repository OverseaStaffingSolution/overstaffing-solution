# Sales Lead Qualification

> Rigorous lead vetting, BANT scoring (Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline), and appointment scheduling for your sales team.

## Key Features
- **Inbound Lead Follow-Up**: Contacting web form submissions within minutes to maximize conversion probability.
- **BANT Qualification Framework**: Assessing prospect fit before transferring to Account Executives.
- **Direct Calendar Integration**: Scheduling meetings directly onto your sales reps' Google Calendar or Outlook.
- **CRM Syncing**: Instant updates to Salesforce, HubSpot, or Pipedrive.

## Ideal Use Cases
- High-volume inbound lead processing.
- B2B sales pipeline acceleration.
- Account Executive time optimization.
